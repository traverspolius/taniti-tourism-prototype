FAQ / Travel Information

Safety and health

Violent crime is rare; petty crime is increasing. Hospital and clinics available.

Money and power

U.S. dollar accepted; many businesses also accept euros and yen; 120-volt outlets.

Alcohol and holidays

The drinking age on Taniti is 18. Alcohol is not sold between midnight and 9 a.m. Attractions and restaurants may close for national holidays.

Language

Many younger Tanitians speak English; less English is spoken in rural areas.

Safety and health

Violent crime is rare; petty crime is increasing. One hospital and several clinics are available; the hospital has many multilingual employees.

Money and power

U.S. dollar accepted; many businesses also accept euros and yen; 120-volt outlets. Banks offer currency exchange, and many businesses accept major credit cards.

Alcohol and holidays

The legal drinking age on Taniti is 18. Alcohol is not sold between midnight and 9 a.m.; many attractions and restaurants close for national holidays. Confirm opening hours when planning your visit.

Language

Many younger Tanitians speak English; less English is spoken in rural areas.

Transportation

View all transportation options.

Most visitors arrive by air. A small cruise ship also docks in Yellow Leaf Bay for one night per week; confirm arrival arrangements with your carrier.

Public and private buses
Public buses serve Taniti City from 5 a.m. to 11 p.m. every day. Private buses serve the rest of the island. Confirm routes and departure times with providers.
Taxis
Taxis are available in Taniti City.
Rental cars
Cars can be rented from a local agency near the airport.
Bicycles
Bicycles and helmets are available to rent from several vendors. Helmets are required by law.
Walking
Taniti City is fairly flat and walkable. The area around Merriton Landing is also easy to explore on foot.

Fares and rental prices: Prices are not provided in the project materials. Confirm current fares and rental rates with the transportation provider.

Transportation details are from the supplied “About the Island” project source.

Getting to Taniti

Air travel

Almost all visitors arrive by air. Taniti has a small airport that can accommodate small jets and propeller planes. For commercial airline service, confirm available flights and arrival arrangements with your carrier; airline names, routes, schedules, and prices are not provided in the project materials.

Cruise visits

Some visitors arrive on a small cruise ship that docks in Yellow Leaf Bay for one night per week. Confirm visit dates and arrangements with your cruise provider.

Getting Around Taniti

Public Buses

Public buses serve Taniti City and operate daily from 5:00 a.m. to 11:00 p.m.

Private Buses

Private buses serve transportation needs outside Taniti City. Confirm routes and departure times with the provider.

Taxis

Taxis are available in Taniti City.

Rental Cars

Visitors who prefer independent transportation can rent a car from a local rental agency near the airport.

Bicycles

Bicycles and helmets are available to rent from several vendors. Helmets are legally required.

Walking

Taniti City is fairly flat and walkable. The area around Merriton Landing is also easy to explore on foot.

Fares and rental prices: Prices are not provided in the project materials. Confirm current fares, rental rates, and availability with the transportation provider.
